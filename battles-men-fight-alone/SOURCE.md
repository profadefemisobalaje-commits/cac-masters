### Proposed book specifications

- **Total chapters:** 45
- **Major parts:** 12
- **Practical appendices:** 20
- **Recommended trim size:** 6 × 9 inches
- **Writing approach:** Narrative nonfiction, psychological insight, practical self-development
- **Intended readership:** Adult men, couples, parents, counselors, educators, and general readers

**Editorial direction:** The book should use compelling, realistic life situations, original illustrative stories, evidence-informed psychological explanations, and practical exercises. Its central message should be that men can carry serious responsibilities without being expected to endure emotional suffering in isolation.

This 45-chapter structure provides a comprehensive foundation for developing *Battles Men Fight Alone* into a substantial, professionally published book by Prof. Adeleke Daniel Durojaiye (PhD).
