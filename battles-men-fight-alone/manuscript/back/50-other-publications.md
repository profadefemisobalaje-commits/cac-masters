**Becoming a Doctor of International Energy and Natural Resources Law**
A study of advanced legal research in the field of international energy and natural resources law, addressing the framework of treaties, institutions, and disputes that govern the exploration, exploitation, and trade of energy and natural resources, and the methods by which a doctoral researcher in the field conducts a substantial contribution to it.
