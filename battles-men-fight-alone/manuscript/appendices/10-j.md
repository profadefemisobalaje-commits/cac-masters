# A Practical Guide to Building Supportive Friendships {.appendix letter="J"}

This appendix is the working material of Part VII and Chapter 45.4. It is written for the man who has concluded that his position is conducted alone and who requires the construction of parties, and it proceeds from the finding that the construction is conducted by the man's own conduct over a period rather than by the search for a friend.

## The Position

Three findings from the case material govern the appendix.

**The first is that men's friendships are frequently conducted through an activity rather than through a conversation.** The finding is not a limitation to be corrected. It is the ordinary mechanism, and the appendix works with it: the friendship is built around a setting, a task, or a recurring occasion, and the conversation is conducted alongside.

**The second is that the parties who are available are frequently already present.** The colleague, the neighbour, the former classmate, the fellow member of the association, the man met at the school gate, the brother-in-law who has been friendly for years. The finding of Chapter 45.2 applies: the absence is frequently maintained by the man's conduct rather than produced by the absence of parties.

**The third is that the construction takes a year and not a week.** The case material records that men who attempt the construction in a month conclude that it has failed, and that men who conduct the same acts over twelve months report that they have three or four parties.

## Stage Two: The Three Parties

The construction requires three parties, and they are different in kind.

**The first is the one person who knows the whole.** The party of Chapter 42.5's second support. The party may be a wife, a brother, a friend, a professional, or a man who has conducted a similar position. The party's function is to hold the accurate position.

**The second is the companion of the ordinary.** The party with whom the man conducts an ordinary activity: the weekly walk, the workshop, the football, the repair, the coffee on Saturday, the drive. The party's function is not the disclosure. It is the ordinary association that prevents the man's withdrawal, and the finding is that this party is frequently the one who notices a change before anybody else.

**The third is the man to whom the reader supplies the instrument.** The party described in Chapter 44.5. The party's function is the reversal of the man's position from a recipient to a contributor, and the finding is that a man who has nobody to assist is frequently a man whose own belonging is fragile.

## The Difficulties

Five difficulties are recorded, with their answers.

**The first difficulty is that the man does not know what to say after the greeting.** The answer is the ordinary question asked in the ordinary setting, and the finding is that men's conversations begin with the task and proceed to the material. The question form is: *what are you working on at present*, *how is the family*, *how is the business*, *what is difficult at the moment*.

**The second difficulty is that the man's schedule does not permit a weekly occasion.** The answer is the reduction of one commitment, which is the material of Chapter 37 and Appendix N, and the finding is that a man who cannot find an hour for a friendship is a man whose position has been examined and found to be conducted entirely for other parties.

**The third difficulty is that the party is not a man who speaks about difficulty.** The answer is the distinction of the three parties above. The companion of the ordinary requires no disclosure, and the finding is that the ordinary association frequently produces the disclosure over months without its being sought.

**The fourth difficulty is that the man has been let down before.** The answer is the material of Chapter 26's betrayal: the party who betrayed the man is one party, the man's response is a general arrangement, and the general arrangement is the thing that costs him. The correction is the specific party, the specific instrument, and the record of what has actually happened rather than of what was feared.

**The fifth difficulty is that the man is in a community where men do not speak.** The answer is the material of Chapter 43.7 and the ordinary form of the work: the office in the setting, the recurring occasion, the small change to an agenda. The finding is that a single man's changed conduct in a small institution frequently alters what is permissible for the men around him.

## The Measures

Four measures apply at the end of the twelve weeks.

1. **The parties.** The number of parties who hold the accurate position, and their names.
2. **The occasions.** The number of recurring occasions conducted in the last month.
3. **The obligations.** The offices accepted and conducted.
4. **The reversal.** The number of parties to whom the reader has supplied an instrument.

The case material's finding is that a man who has one party who holds the whole position, two recurring occasions, and one obligation that he conducts has constructed belonging in the sense of Chapter 45.4, and that further construction is not required.
