# Personal Financial Recovery Planning Worksheet {.appendix letter="D"}

This appendix is the working instrument for the financial material of Parts III and XI and Chapter 42. It is designed for a man who has concluded that his financial position must be stated, reduced, and restructured. Its purpose is not to supply financial advice, which requires a qualified practitioner who has seen the reader's documents, and to allow the reader to arrive at that practitioner with an accurate position rather than an account of one.

## Before Beginning

Three notes apply.

The first is that the worksheet requires documents. The reader should gather the bank statements for the last three months, the statements of every facility and loan, the salary or income records, the school fee arrangements, the rent or mortgage documents, the guarantees he has signed, and the record of any obligation to which he is a party. A worksheet completed from memory will be inaccurate in the item that matters most.

The second is that the worksheet is conducted twice: once alone, and once with the party of the household, in the form of Chapter 42.6's third step. The first conduct is the man's accurate position. The second is the household's.

The third is that the reader who discovers a guarantee, a loan, or an obligation that he had not recorded should treat that discovery as the worksheet's principal output, and should engage the professional route before conducting any further borrowing.

## Part Five: The Recovery Plan

The plan is conducted in the order that Chapter 42.6 supplies: the statement, the one person, the household, the professionals, the reductions in three layers, and the reconstruction at the true level.

### The Statement

Write, in five sentences, the position as it actually is: the income, the expenditure, the obligations, the concealed total, and the margin. This is the paragraph the reader will use in the household conversation and at the first professional appointment.

### The Reconstruction at the True Level

Record the position as it will be conducted at its true level: the income that the household will actually live on, the obligations that will be serviced, the reductions that will become permanent, the obligations that will be settled, and the date at which the position is expected to be stable. The finding of the case material is that the true level is generally more productive than the performance, because the interest, the penalties, the avoided decisions, and the vigilance are removed.

## Part Six: The Rules

Six rules govern the worksheet's use.

1. **No new borrowing to cover an existing obligation without a professional's written view.** The rule addresses the escalation described in Chapter 42.3's third mechanism.
2. **The concealed total is stated to one party within seven days.** The reader may choose the party and the professional is preferable where the total is large.
3. **The household's participation is not optional where the household is affected.** The finding of Chapter 42.7 is that the household's capacity is frequently greater than the man assumes and that the concealment is more damaging than the figure.
4. **The reductions are conducted in layers and not in a single act.** The single act is frequently the house or the school fees, and its conduct in the first week is the most frequent cause of the plan's collapse.
5. **The record is monthly.** The position is restated on the first of each month, in figures, and the restatement is what distinguishes a recovery from a resolution.
6. **The professional's advice is recorded and followed or, where it is not followed, the reason is recorded.** The rule protects the reader from the position in which the professional's advice was declined and the reason was not examined.
