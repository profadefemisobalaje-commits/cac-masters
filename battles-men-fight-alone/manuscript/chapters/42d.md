## 42.5 The Six Supports

The chapter turns to its constructive material. A life that does not depend on pretending is not a life of full disclosure to everybody, and the section establishes what it is: **six supports, each of which removes a specific pretence's necessity.**

### Support One: A Position Stated in Figures, to the Man Himself

**The material.** The written statement of Chapter 38.4, in its six sections, held current. The income, the obligations, the assets, the household's requirements, the capacities, and the standing's obligations, recorded as figures.

**The mechanism.** The support removes the necessity of the script, because a man who knows his position in figures is not required to compose his answers to the questions about it. The case material's finding: the man's ability to state his position in figures is the chapter's single most useful instrument, and its absence is the sign of Section 42.4's first item.

**The frequency.** The statement is conducted monthly, and it takes under an hour once its form is established.

### Support Two: One Person Who Knows the Whole

**The material.** The disclosure function of Chapter 27.4, in the form the case material establishes as the minimum: one person who has the entire account of the man's position, including the material that is concealed from every other party, and including the material he is ashamed of.

**The mechanism.** The support removes the necessity of the isolation and the possibility of an unbroken concealment, because a man who has stated the whole of his position to one party cannot be conducting a full pretence. The finding: the party should be selected for the capacity to hold the material rather than for convenience, and the material of Chapter 27.4's five sources and Chapter 35.4's four parts applies.

**The specification.** The person's name should be written, the arrangement's frequency stated, and the material's subject matter stated: the position, the money, the work, the health, the marriage, and the decisions the man is inclined to take too quickly. The material of Chapter 38.2's fourth task applies in full.

### Support Three: The Household's Arrangements Stated to the Household

**The material.** The four arrangements of Chapter 38.6 and Chapter 40.6's Matter One: the disclosure, the household's own arrangements, the household's role, and the household's own people and rest.

**The mechanism.** The support removes the money, work, household, and marriage domains' pretences at the household level, which is the level at which the case material records the greatest cost. The specific finding: the household that knows the position is a household that participates in its management, and its members' arrangements are constructed on the actual position, which is the only form in which the arrangements can hold.

**The specification.** The monthly statement of the position, in figures, at a stated occasion; the household's requirements stated and its reductions conducted as arrangements rather than as impositions; and the household's account of what the position requires of each member sought rather than supplied.

### Support Four: The Reserve

**The material.** The material of Chapter 23.4's financial arrangements and Appendix D: the reserve that stands between the man's position and the collapse. The case material's specification: an amount equal to the household's fixed requirements for a stated period, held in a form that the man can access within days.

**The mechanism.** The support removes the trigger of the escalation, which is the specific mechanism of Section 42.2's second cost. The finding is mechanical and it is the chapter's most concrete: **the man with a reserve does not take the facility, and the man without one does.** The case material records the finding in the men's own accounts: the borrowing that began the collapse was taken because the wages, the fees, or the instalment fell due in a week in which no reserve existed.

**The specification.** The reserve's amount stated as a figure; the period it covers stated; the form it is held in stated; and the rule for its use stated: the reserve is used for the fixed requirements and is not available for the occasions, the appearance, the instalments, or the contributions.

### Support Five: The Man's Own Capacity and Its Documentation

**The material.** The material of Chapters 23.4, 24.5, and 40.5's capacities: the qualification, the competence, the registration, the practice, the trade, the record, the network, and the second source of income. And its documentation, which is the support's mechanical requirement: the certificate, the registration, the written record of the man's work, the references, the portfolio, the account of what he has done.

**The mechanism.** The support removes the work domain's pretence by removing its necessity. The man whose position is a single employment, a single client, a single contract, or a single competence that is represented rather than held is in the position in which a change in the position requires a concealment. The man with a documented second capacity has an alternative that can be stated, which is the finding: **the ability to state a true alternative removes the need to conceal a false position.**

**The specification.** The second capacity named; the documentation's state recorded; the frequency with which it is maintained stated; and the income it could produce at the man's actual position estimated in figures.

### Support Six: The Standing That Does Not Require Performance

**The material.** The material of Chapters 25.6 and 41.2's fifth clause: the standing that is produced by the man's actual conduct rather than by his presentation, and the reduction of the domain that is conducted for the audience.

**The mechanism.** The support removes the standing and appearance domains' pretences, which are the ones whose maintenance consumes the man's income. The finding from the case material: the standing produced by the man's actual competence, reliability, and conduct survives the disclosure of his position, and the standing produced by his presentation does not. The specific finding: the men who conducted a controlled disclosure generally retained their professional standing, and the men who were exposed lost it, and the difference is the mechanism's demonstration.

**The specification.** The domains in which the man's standing is actual, recorded with evidence; the domains in which it is presented, recorded with their annual cost; the reductions, with the form of Chapter 33.6's three releases; and the man's own measure, from Chapter 41.5's fourth part, stated with items the audience cannot see.

### The Supports in Combination

The finding the section closes with, and it is the one the case material supports most strongly: **the six supports are cumulative and their order is not arbitrary.**

The position in figures is conducted first, because it is the instrument of the other five. The person who knows the whole is conducted second, because the man's conduct of the statement requires the arrangement in which it is held. The household's arrangements are conducted third, since the household's participation is what the remaining supports require. The reserve is conducted fourth, because it is what prevents the escalation while the position is being addressed. The documented capacity is conducted fifth, since it is the ground on which the man's alternatives are constructed. The standing is conducted last, because the man's authority to reduce the standing's domain is generally produced by the other five.

## 42.6 The Dismantling

The chapter's practical section. A pretence is not removed by a decision; it is dismantled in a sequence, and the sequence is the one the practice material supports. The section supplies the five steps, their order, and the errors that interrupt them.

### The Order and Its Reason

The order is: **the statement; the one person; the household; the professionals; the reductions; the reconstruction at the true level.**

The order's reason is mechanical, and it is the same reason as Chapter 38.5's: each step produces the condition the next requires. The statement produces the man's own knowledge of his position, without which he cannot state it to anybody. The one person produces the arrangement in which the statement can be delivered and revised. The household's disclosure produces the parties whose participation the reductions require. The professionals produce the arrangements with the creditors, the employers, the lawyers, the accountants, and the clinicians. The reductions produce the position at its true level. The reconstruction produces the life that does not require the pretence.

The errors that interrupt the sequence are the seven of Chapter 38.3, and the three most frequently recorded in this material are the attempt to conduct the household's disclosure before the statement exists, the attempt to conduct the reductions before the professionals have been engaged, and the attempt to reconstruct the position at a new level through a further appearance.

### Step One: The Statement

**The conduct.** Chapter 38.4's six sections, in figures, completed in writing. The reader should note the specific instruction from Chapter 38.2: no decision of consequence is taken before the statement exists, and the statement takes days rather than weeks.

**The specific additions for this chapter.** The statement's sections are extended by the two columns of Section 42.1: the fact, and the parties who must not learn it. And by the four mechanisms of Section 42.3, with their monthly cost recorded. The extension's purpose is that the statement should record not only the position but the position's concealment, which is the item the reader is dismantling.

### Step Two: The One Person

**The conduct.** The selection of the party and the delivery of the statement to them, in the four-part form of Chapter 34.6's disclosure: the position; the man's own part in it; what has been concealed and from whom; and what he intends to conduct.

**The specific finding.** The case material records the finding that this step is the one at which the man's position alters, and that the alteration is frequently visible to him rather than to the party: *I said it out loud to one man and I slept for seven hours, and I had not slept for seven hours in a year.*

**The instruction.** The party is selected for the capacity rather than the convenience; the statement is read rather than recited; and the man's request of the party is stated specifically: the holding of the material, the asking of the question at a stated interval, and the naming of the decisions the man is inclined to take too quickly.

### Step Three: The Household

**The conduct.** The four arrangements of Chapter 38.6, in the order supplied there, with the statement in hand.

**The specific finding for this chapter.** The household's disclosure is the step the case material records as the one the man most fears and the one that most frequently produces the position's recovery. The specific finding: the household's response is generally to the arrangement rather than to the fact, and the fact that the household most frequently identifies as the difficulty is not the size of the position but its concealment's duration.

**The instruction.** The disclosure is conducted before the reductions, so that the household's arrangements are constructed on the true position rather than on the reductions' consequences.

### Step Four: The Professionals

**The conduct.** The engagement of the routes required by the statement: the insolvency or debt practitioner, the accountant, the lawyer for the guarantees, the contract, the property, and the family arrangements, the clinician for the concealed health matter, the marriage or relationship route where the marriage's domain is concerned, and the employment or career advice.

**The specific finding.** The professionals' engagement is the step at which the position's size generally reduces, and the mechanism is the one supplied in Chapter 38.8: the position becomes a matter of figures held by a party who is not ashamed of it, and the arrangements available to a professional are generally not available to the man conducting the same negotiations privately.

**The instruction.** The professional is engaged before the reductions, and the reason is the one the case material records: the reductions conducted without the professionals are frequently conducted in the wrong order — the appearance, the occasion, and the contributions maintained while the Category A obligations are unpaid — which is the triage error of Chapter 38.3 and Chapter 38.4's third section.

### Step Five: The Reductions, in Three Layers

**The conduct.** The reductions are conducted in layers, from the least visible to the most, and the reason is the one the case material records: the man's available authority for the reductions is produced by the earlier layers' effectiveness.

**The first layer: the items nobody sees.** The subscriptions, the memberships, the arrangements conducted from inertia, the second vehicle, the additional engagement's hours, the hospitality, the unnecessary travel, the consumption of Chapter 40.5's Category Four. The finding: this layer generally produces a reduction of between five and fifteen per cent of the household's requirements, and it is conducted without any disclosure to any party outside the household.

**The second layer: the items some people see.** The occasions' scale, the contributions' level, the community's arrangement, the association's office, the extended family's arrangement, the school's arrangements, the vehicle's replacement. Each is conducted with a disclosure to the specific party concerned, and Chapter 40.6's Matter Two supplies the form: the notice, the arrangement, the party's capacity, and the resistance endured rather than argued with.

**The third layer: the items everybody sees.** The house, the address, the employment, the business's form, the school, the marriage's public arrangements, the position. Each is conducted with the household's participation and with the professional route, and each is conducted last, because the man's authority to conduct it and the family's capacity to absorb it are produced by the earlier layers.

### Step Six: The Reconstruction at the True Level

**The conduct.** The material of Chapter 38.5's stages and Chapter 40's audit, conducted at the position's actual level rather than at the represented level.

**The specific finding, and the section closes with it.** The case material records the finding consistently: **the position conducted at its true level is generally more productive than the position conducted behind the pretence.** The mechanism's components are four. **The first:** the vigilance's cost is removed, and its material — the attention, the sleep, the hours, the decisions — returns to the man's actual work. **The second:** the escalation's cost is removed, and the interest, the instalments, and the arrangements' margins stop consuming the income. **The third:** the decisions are taken on the actual position, and the case material records the improvement in their quality as the finding that the men reported as the most unexpected: *I began to make decisions that worked, because I had stopped making them for a position I was pretending to be in.* **The fourth:** the man's relationships are restored to their actual parties, and the disclosure function of Chapter 27 is available, with the consequences of Chapters 31 and 35.
