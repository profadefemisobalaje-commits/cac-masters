## 41.6 The Substitution

The analysis of a definition does not alter it, and the substitution is the section that supplies the construction. Its method is the replacement of each clause by specific conduct, conducted in the reader's actual circumstances, at the level of the act.

### The Four Steps of the Substitution

**Step one: the clause is stated.** In the man's own words, as he holds it, without softening.

**Step two: the acts are listed.** The specific acts the clause currently requires of him, in his actual circumstances.

**Step three: the acts are tested.** For each act: what it produces, whom it serves, and what it costs. The criterion for retention: the act produces a good that the man can state, in his own voice, without the definition's assistance. The criterion for release: the act produces nothing but compliance.

**Step four: the replacement act is written.** Specific, dated, conducted in the man's circumstances, and stated in the form of a first step.

### The Twelve Substitutions

The following substitutions are the ones most frequently recorded in the case material. The reader should construct his own and should use these as the patterns of the form rather than as the content.

**1. From *I do not discuss the position with my wife* to *I state the position's size on the first Sunday of each month, in figures, and I ask what it requires of her arrangements.***

**2. From *I do not ask for help* to *I have four named parties I telephone before I hold a difficulty for more than a month, and their names are written.***

**3. From *I do not show that it is affecting me* to *I state what is present in me to my wife within the day, in four sentences, and I do not require her to resolve it.***

**4. From *I do not cry* to *I permit the tears when they arrive, and I do not apologise for them.***

**5. From *I do not complain* to *I state the load's size as information, without the complaint and without the concealment.***

**6. From *I handle my own problems* to *I conduct the position and I use every instrument, including the professional and the man who has been where I am.***

**7. From *I control my household* to *I answer for the household's position, I distribute the decisions, and I state my determination as a position rather than as an order.***

**8. From *a man's word is the decision* to *my word is my undertaking, and my household's members are parties to the arrangements that concern them.***

**9. From *I do not take a day off* to *I keep the period of rest, and if there is one who cannot rest it is on the strength of my rest.***

**10. From *I am measured by what I own and what I have achieved* to *I am measured by my whole conduct, and I can state three items in that measure which the audience cannot see.***

**11. From *I do not become attached to that extent* to *I am attached, I say so, and the attachment is what I am for in this house.***

**12. From *I must be the equal of the men around me* to *I conduct my household's position at the level of my own obligations, and the comparison is not among my criteria.***

### The Three Requirements of the Substitution

**Requirement one: each replacement is in acts and in the present circumstances.** Not *I will be more open*, but the party, the occasion, the sentence, and the frequency. The case material is consistent: the substitutions that were conducted were stated as acts and the substitutions that were abandoned were stated as intentions.

**Requirement two: the first step is conducted within the week.** The finding from the case material: the man who conducts one substitution within a week of the exercise conducts the majority of the remainder, and the man who completes the exercise without conducting one does not return to it.

**Requirement three: the substitution is stated to one person.** The material of Chapters 35, 39.5's Practice Five, and 40.6's Matter One. The reading of the man's statement to the ally or to the household is the mechanism by which the definition is altered in the man's actual arrangements rather than in his notebook.

### The Three Difficulties

**Difficulty one: the substitution reads as a betrayal of the man's father.** The difficulty is reported by men more frequently than any other in this material, and the chapter's instruction is specific: **the reader is not replacing his father; he is completing him.** The case material's formulation, recorded from a reader in a group setting: *My father gave me the rules for the conditions he was in, and he did not have the information that I have, and I am not judging him. I am adding the part he could not have known about, and I am doing it for his grandsons.* The instruction's second element: the man's own addition is the more useful because his father's conduct is not available to be altered and the man's conduct is.

**Difficulty two: the substitution feels like a description of a different man.** The difficulty of the man whose definition is not a set of clauses but a constitution. The instruction is the substitution's size: the reader replaces two or three clauses rather than the whole, and the case material records that a man who conducts two substitutions has altered his definition's effect on his household within a year.

**Difficulty three: the household does not believe the change.** The material of Chapter 39.6's second rule and Chapter 40.6's Matter Two: the change is conducted rather than announced; the household's doubt is endured rather than argued with; and the conduct is continued for the period in which the household is calibrating its response, which the case material records as generally between six and eighteen months.

## 41.7 Illustrative Case: The Two Definitions in One Man

::: casebox
**Composite case.** Constructed for teaching purposes from patterns reported in men's counselling, occupational practice, and family practice.

**Ibrahim is forty-seven, a police officer of the rank of chief superintendent, married to Halima for twenty-two years, with three children aged twenty, seventeen, and eleven.**

Ibrahim's position in the case is the one this chapter describes in its most explicit form, because his professional definition and his household's requirements were stated to him in writing and in conduct, and the case records both.

**The professional definition**, as he described it in the first sitting: *In my service the rule is simple. You do not show fear and you do not show favour and you do not show that a thing has touched you. I have been in the service for twenty-four years and I have never once said to a colleague that something was difficult for me, and I have never once been asked. The rank is the thing. When I put on the uniform I am the rank, and the rank does not have a private position.*

**The household's material**, as described by Halima in a separate interview: *He is a good man. He has never touched me in anger. He provides. He is respected everywhere. And for twenty-two years he has come into this house and he has been the rank. He answers me the way he answers a constable. He asks me questions when he wants a decision. When my father died in 2018 he arranged the burial and he stood at the gate greeting the people for three days and in the evening of the third day he asked me whether the accounts were complete. I said yes and I went inside and I sat in the bathroom for one hour. I have never told him that either.*

The material of the case that produced the man's attendance was a specific event in his twenty-fourth year of service. He was required, as the senior officer present at the scene of a fatal road collision, to identify the body of a young man and to inform the family. The young man was nineteen years old, and the man's own son was seventeen at the time. The event, in his own account: *I did everything correctly. I made the identification. I went to the house. I told the father in four sentences, the way I have been trained, and I stood there while he fell down, and I held his arm and I said the two sentences I always say, and I came back to the station and I wrote my report and I went home and I ate my food and I went to sleep at ten. And I woke at twenty past two in the morning and I could not move my arms. I lay there for about an hour and I thought, I have been doing this for twenty-four years and I have never felt any of it, and it is not that I am strong, it is that I have not been in the room. And I thought about my son, and I began to shake, and I could not stop it, and I got out of the bed and I stood in the bathroom and I did not know what to do and I did not wake my wife because I did not know how to tell her that I was frightened.*

The work, conducted over about two years, had four elements.

**The first element: the definition written down.** Ibrahim wrote eleven statements, and the ones he identified as his own, in his own words, were: *a man is the rank; a man does not show that a thing has touched him; a man handles it alone; a man's household is conducted by him; a man does not explain himself twice; and a man is the one who stands at the gate.*

**The second element: the eight tests.** The tests' findings, in his own account, were three. **The first** was Test Two, the possibility: *The counsellor said, can a man arrange not to be touched by a dead boy. I said that a man can arrange not to show it. She said, and you have arranged that, and the touching is still happening, and it is happening in your bathroom at half past two. I have never forgotten that sentence.* **The second** was Test Five, the omissions, in which he identified two: *There is nothing in my rules about what happens after, and nothing at all about my own house. It is all about the station.* **The third** was Test Eight, the evidence, and it concerned his own father. His father had been a soldier and had died when Ibrahim was twenty-six, and the material was: *I never had one conversation with my father that was not about my career or the family. And when he was dying, in the hospital, I sat with him for six days and I wanted him to say one thing to me about himself, anything, and he told me about the arrangements for his burial and the house in Kaduna. I have been angry about that for twenty-two years and I have been doing it to my own children.*

**The third element: the household's material.** Ibrahim conducted the disclosure to Halima in the fourth month, in the four-part form, and the passages he supplied included the bathroom conversation: *I told her about the three days of her father's burial and about what I had asked her, and she began to cry, and I sat there and after some time I put my hand on her shoulder, which I have not done in about ten years, and I said the sentence that the counsellor had told me to say if I could not find my own, which was that I was sorry and that I did not know how to do this. And she said something that I will not forget. She said, Ibrahim, I have been waiting for you to be bad at something for twenty-two years. You have never once been bad at anything.*

**The fourth element: the substitutions.** He selected three, and his own formulations, recorded from his written statement, were the following. **One:** *From, I am the rank in this house, to, I put the rank at the gate.* He described the conduct: *I decided to take off the tunic at the door. I am not making a joke. I would enter the house and I would stand in the small corridor at the back and I would take off the uniform and I would put on the cloth and I would come into the house and I would speak in my ordinary voice, and I did it for about four months before it was not a performance. My youngest noticed before anybody. He said, Baba, you are talking like a person.* **Two:** *From, I do not explain myself twice, to, I tell them what I am thinking and I let them disagree.* The conduct: *I began to say what I was thinking at the table. I said, the thing about this matter is that I do not know what to do. My wife stopped eating. My daughter said, Baba, you do not know what to do? I said, no, and she said, that has never happened in this house. And then they began to tell me things. My second one told me in that week about a matter at the school that she had been carrying for four months and had not told me because I would have gone to the school and settled it and she did not want it settled, she wanted to be heard. I listened for about forty minutes. I have never listened for forty minutes in that house.* **Three:** *From, a man is the one who stands at the gate, to, a man is the one who can be found inside the house.* The conduct: *I delegated the gate. I mean this literally. At my niece's wedding in the following year I stood at the gate for one hour and then I went inside and I sat with my family and I ate the food. My brother-in-law asked me who was at the gate and I said, my brother is at the gate, and he looked at me for a moment and then he laughed.*

The review, conducted at eighteen months and recorded in the case document, contained three items. **First:** the rank and the household were being conducted separately, and Ibrahim's own description of the change was: *The service has not changed and I have not changed my work. I have my rank. What has changed is that there is one room in this town in which I am not the rank, and I go to that room every evening, and I am fifty years old and I have just found out that I like my wife's company and that my daughter tells jokes.* **Second:** his sleep, which had been the presenting difficulty: *I sleep. I do not know why. I have not done anything about the sleep. The counsellor says it is because there is nowhere left in the day in which nothing is happening.* **Third:** the material of his own father, which he described as follows: *I went to the grave in December with a bottle of water and I poured it on the ground and I said, Baba, I have added something to what you gave me, and I have given it to your grandchildren, and you were doing what you knew. That was all I said and I stood there for about ten minutes and then I drove home and my son was in the compound and he said, Baba, where have you been, and I told him.*
:::

Five features of this case deserve isolation.

**The two definitions were explicit and both were written down.** The professional definition was stated in the man's own words, and the household's requirement was stated by his wife. The case is included because the reader's own two definitions are generally as distinct as Ibrahim's and as rarely examined together: the definition a man conducts at work, where it functions, and the definition he conducts in his household, where its extension is not required.

**The wakefulness at twenty past two was the moment of the examination.** The event was a decade of the man's professional experience compressed into one night, and the case records the mechanism of the chapter's fifth omission: the definition supplies no instruction about what happens after. The case is included because the material of this book's examination is generally located at a single occasion of this kind, and because the man's own description — *it is not that I am strong, it is that I have not been in the room* — is the chapter's central finding in the reader's own voice.

**The tests produced the material rather than the counsellor.** Tests Two, Five, and Eight supplied the possibility's finding, the omissions, and the father's conversation in the hospital. The case is included because the chapter's instrument is analytical and its results are the reader's own, and because the man's own evidence about his father — carried for twenty-two years and reproduced in his own household — is the third mechanism of Section 41.3 in its clearest available form.

**The substitutions were specific, physical, and conducted.** The tunic on the corridor hook, the statement at the table that he did not know what to do, and the hour at his niece's wedding inside rather than at the gate. The case is included because the substitutions' forms are the chapter's practical instruction: a definition is altered by conduct that is small, repeated, and visible, and the man's own report — *I did it for about four months before it was not a performance* — records the period honestly.

**The household's response was immediate and it contained the material the man had not had.** The wife's sentence about being bad at something, and the daughter's matter at the school carried for four months. The case is included because it records the finding of Chapters 31.1 and 34.6 in the household's setting: the man's definition has been reducing the information available to him, and the reduction's removal produces the material within a week.
