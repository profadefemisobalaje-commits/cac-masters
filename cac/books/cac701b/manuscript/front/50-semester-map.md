# The Semester at a Glance {.front}

Sixteen weeks, one lecture unit each. Weeks 1–14 teach the course; week 15 integrates it on a single extended case; week 16 prepares the examination. The right-hand column names the assessed work of each week; the criteria for each are printed inside the week.

| Week | Title | Principal lineage or task | Assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mapping Development | the four questions; methods; ethics; the child in context | Reading response, 600 words |
| 2 | Foundations Before Birth | genes, teratogens, epigenetics, early adversity | Teratogen case analysis |
| 3 | The Constructing Child | Piaget: sensorimotor and preoperational thought | Conservation demonstration log |
| 4 | The Social Mind | Vygotsky: ZPD, scaffolding, private speech | Scaffolding exercise |
| 5 | Processing and Executive Function | attention, working memory, theory of mind | False-belief critique, 800 words |
| 6 | Temperament, Attachment, Regulation | goodness of fit; emotion regulation | Goodness-of-fit analysis |
| 7 | Language and Communication | milestones, bilingualism, red flags | Language sample analysis |
| 8 | Self, Identity, Morality | Erikson, Kohlberg, Gilligan, conscience | Moral reasoning interview |
| 9 | Peers, Play and Gender | Parten, friendship, gender theories | Play observation record |
| 10 | Family, Culture, Adversity, Resilience | parenting styles, ACEs, ordinary magic | Adversity and resilience map |
| 11 | Atypical Development I | neurodevelopmental presentations, developmentally read | Developmental timeline case |
| 12 | Atypical Development II | developmental psychopathology: equifinality, transaction | Equifinality essay, 1,200 words |
| 13 | Integration | the developing child in the counselling room | Formulation portfolio |
| 14 | Observation, Screening, Measurement | tools, cultural fairness, outcome measurement | Screening critique |
| 15 | The Practicum: Three Ages | case conference across toddler, school-age, adolescent | Practicum presentation |
| 16 | Revision and Examination | consolidation, past questions, examination technique | Final examination |

**Notional hours.** 1.6 units ≈ 24 notional learning hours across the sixteen weeks, of which roughly one third is scheduled teaching and two thirds your own preparation, observation and writing. Weeks vary: the practicum week is heavier in supervised work, the revision week lighter in teaching.

**Reading load.** Each week marks one core text among its six readings. Read the core text before the week's teaching and the remainder across the week. The core texts of the course are Berk's *Child Development*, Santrock's *Child Development*, and Shonkoff and Phillips's *From Neurons to Neighborhoods*.
