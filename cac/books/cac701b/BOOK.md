## Scope of this volume

A complete lecture course in the theories and evidence of human development from conception
through the school years, written for counsellors of children and adolescents: the questions and
methods of developmental science; foundations laid before birth; Piaget and the constructing
child; Vygotsky and the social mind; information processing and executive function; temperament,
attachment and the regulation of emotion; language and communication; self, identity and moral
development; peers, play and gender; family, culture, adversity and resilience; atypical
development and the principles of developmental psychopathology; and the integration of all of
this into the developmental formulation of a child in the counselling room.

Sixteen weeks: fourteen teaching weeks, an integration and practicals week, and a revision and
examination week.
