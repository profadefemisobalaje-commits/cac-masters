# The Semester at a Glance {.front}

Sixteen weeks, four hours of contact each: fourteen teaching weeks, a practicum week and a
revision-and-examination week. Every week carries outcomes, key concepts, three lectures, at least
one purpose-drawn diagram, worked dilemmas, a practical, seminar questions, an assessed task and a
reading list. The code of conduct referred to throughout is your registering body's; where this
volume states a rule, it states the rule most codes share, and where jurisdictions differ, it says
so and tells you to check.

The four strands gather as: **Foundations** (weeks 1–4), the principles and the three great
duties; **The Practitioner** (weeks 5–8), the self as instrument and its limits; **The Profession**
(weeks 9–12), the counsellor among institutions and injustices; **Practice** (weeks 13–16), the
decision model, the law, the panel and the examination. Cases from volumes 2 and 3 of this series
return here as closed case material, with consent imagined and labelled fictitious, so that ethics
is taught on clients you already know.
