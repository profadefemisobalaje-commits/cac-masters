## Scope of this volume

A complete lecture course in the development of the adolescent, written for counsellors of
children and adolescents and sized for a sixteen-week semester: what adolescence is and how
it was invented; puberty and its timing; the adolescent brain and the sleep that reshapes it;
adolescent thinking, egocentrism and decision-making under heat; identity and its statuses;
the self, esteem and the body under the comparison machine; emotion, internalising difficulty
and the conversations that save; the family renegotiated; peers, romance and sexual
development; school, work and aspiration; risk, resilience and positive youth development;
adolescence across cultures and adversities; clinical onsets and the adolescent formulation;
and the practice of engaging, confiding with and measuring outcomes with adolescents.

Sixteen weeks: fourteen teaching weeks, an integration and practicals week, and a revision
and examination week.
