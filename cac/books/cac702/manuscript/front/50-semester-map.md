# The Semester at a Glance {.front}

Sixteen weeks, one lecture unit each. Weeks 1–14 teach the course; week 15 integrates it on a single extended case; week 16 prepares the examination. The right-hand column names the assessed work of each week; the criteria for each are printed inside the week.

| Week | Title | Principal lineage or task | Assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What Is Adolescence? | definitions, histories, the four questions | Reading response, 600 words |
| 2 | Puberty I: Bodies and Timing | the axis, the stages, the clocks | Timing analysis of a case |
| 3 | Puberty II: The Adolescent Brain | dual systems, pruning, the perfect storm of sleep | Dual-systems critique, 800 words |
| 4 | Thinking in Adolescence | formal operations, egocentrism, hot and cold cognition | Decision case analysis |
| 5 | Identity | Erikson, Marcia, ethnic and religious identity | Identity-status interview |
| 6 | Self, Esteem and the Body | trajectories, the comparison machine | Self-concept map |
| 7 | Emotion and Internalising | the gender gap, self-harm, the conversations that save | Risk-conversation log |
| 8 | The Family Renegotiated | autonomy, conflict, monitoring versus snooping | Conflict transcript analysis |
| 9 | Peers, Romance, Sexual Development | cliques, co-rumination, peer influence | Peer-ethnography note |
| 10 | School, Work and Aspiration | engagement, dropout, career development | Aspirations interview |
| 11 | Risk, Resilience, Positive Youth Development | function of risk-taking, the five Cs | Five-Cs audit |
| 12 | Adolescence Across Cultures | rites, child marriage, WEIRD critique | Critical review, 1,200 words |
| 13 | Clinical Adolescence | onsets, formulation, self-harm assessment | Formulation portfolio |
| 14 | Practice with Adolescents | engagement, confidentiality, motivational interviewing | Recorded interviewing segment |
| 15 | The Practicum: Three Adolescents | case conference at 13, 15 and 17 | Practicum presentation |
| 16 | Revision and Examination | consolidation, past questions, examination technique | Final examination |

**Notional hours.** 1.6 units ≈ 24 notional learning hours across the sixteen weeks, of which roughly one third is scheduled teaching and two thirds your own preparation, observation and writing. Weeks vary: the practicum week is heavier in supervised work, the revision week lighter in teaching.

**Reading load.** Each week marks one core text among its six readings. Read the core text before the week's teaching and the remainder across the week. The core texts of the course are Steinberg's *Age of Opportunity*, the *Lancet* commission on adolescent health and wellbeing (Patton and colleagues), and Sawyer and colleagues on the age of adolescence.
