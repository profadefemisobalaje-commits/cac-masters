# The Semester at a Glance {.front}

Sixteen weeks, one lecture unit each. Weeks 1–14 teach the course; week 15 integrates it on a single extended case; week 16 prepares the examination. The right-hand column names the assessed work of each week; the criteria for each are printed inside the week.

| Week | Title | Principal lineage or task | Assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What a Theory Is For | the four questions; mechanism and technique; common factors | Comparative formulation, 1,500 words |
| 2 | The Psychodynamic Lineage I | Freud, Anna Freud, play as communication | Reading response, 600 words |
| 3 | The Psychodynamic Lineage II | attachment, the Strange Situation, the secure base | Attachment analysis of a vignette |
| 4 | The Psychodynamic Lineage III | mentalisation and reflective function | Reflective-function exercise |
| 5 | The Behavioural Lineage I | learning theory, contingency, functional analysis | Functional analysis of a case |
| 6 | The Behavioural Lineage II | cognitive therapy with children and adolescents | Cognitive formulation |
| 7 | The Behavioural Lineage III | third-wave: ACT, DBT-A, mindfulness-based work | Practical log |
| 8 | The Humanistic Lineage I | person-centred: the conditions and the child | Recorded micro-practice |
| 9 | The Humanistic Lineage II | existential and meaning work with adolescents | Essay, 1,200 words |
| 10 | The Systemic Lineage I | Bateson to Minuchin: structure, boundaries, function | Family map of a vignette |
| 11 | The Systemic Lineage II | solution-focused and narrative approaches | Re-authoring exercise |
| 12 | Critique | multicultural, feminist and decolonial readings of theory | Critical review, 1,200 words |
| 13 | Integration I | case formulation across competing accounts | Formulation portfolio |
| 14 | Integration II | evidence-based practice and outcome monitoring | Outcome-measure critique |
| 15 | The Comparative Practicum | one extended case, four formulations, one recommendation | Practicum presentation |
| 16 | Revision and Examination | consolidation, past questions, examination technique | Final examination |

**Notional hours.** 1.6 units ≈ 24 notional learning hours across the sixteen weeks, of which roughly one third is scheduled teaching and two thirds your own preparation, practice and writing. Weeks vary: the practicum week is heavier in supervised work, the revision week lighter in teaching.

**Reading load.** Each week marks one core text among its six readings. Read the core text before the week's teaching and the remainder across the week. The core texts of the course are Corey's *Theory and Practice of Counseling and Psychotherapy*, Geldard, Geldard and Foo's *Counselling Children*, and Weisz and Kazdin's *Evidence-Based Psychotherapies for Children and Adolescents*.
