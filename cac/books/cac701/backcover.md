A complete lecture course in the theories that inform counselling with children and
adolescents, sized for a sixteen-week semester and written to be taught from: objectives,
three lectures, worked examples, practicals, an assessed task with its criteria, and a
reading list for every week.

Four lineages are studied in their own terms and then held to account — what each sees,
what each forbids, and what it cannot see — before the volume turns to integration:
formulation with a named mechanism, a plan with a dated falsifier, outcomes measured
session by session, and the child's own world investigated throughout.

**In this volume**

- the four questions, mechanism and technique, and the running case of Daniel, nine
- the psychodynamic and attachment lineage: play, the secure base, mentalisation
- the behavioural, cognitive and third-wave lineage: from functional analysis to acceptance
- the humanistic and existential lineage: the conditions, and meaning in adversity
- the systemic, solution-focused and narrative lineage: structure, loops, re-authoring
- critique: culture, power, and the provenance of every claim in the volume
- integration: formulation, evidence, outcome monitoring, and the comparative practicum

Twenty-six diagrams drawn for this edition. All case material is fictitious and labelled
as such. Citations in APA 7th edition. British spelling. Conforms to PDF/UA-1.
