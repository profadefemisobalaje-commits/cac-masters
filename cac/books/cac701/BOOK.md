## Scope of this volume

A complete lecture course in the theories that inform counselling with children and adolescents:
what a theory is for; the psychodynamic and attachment lineage; the behavioural, cognitive and
third-wave lineage; the humanistic, person-centred and existential lineage; the systemic and family
lineage; solution-focused and narrative approaches; multicultural and decolonial critique; and the
integration of all of these into evidence-based, formulation-driven practice.

Sixteen weeks: fourteen teaching weeks, an integration and practicals week, and a revision and
examination week.
