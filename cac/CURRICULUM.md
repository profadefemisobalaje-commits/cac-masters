## Queries for the registrar's office — resolve before the handbook is finalised

The source list is reproduced exactly as supplied. Five features of it cannot be resolved by an
editor and must be confirmed by the institution, because each one determines how a chapter is
numbered, how a transcript reads, or how a student plans a semester.

1. **`CAC. 701` is used twice in the 1st Semester**, for two different courses: *Fundamentals of
   Counseling Theories* and *Child Development Theories*. A code that identifies two courses cannot
   appear on a transcript unambiguously. One of the two entries presumably carries a different code.
   *Until resolved, the handbook prints both entries as supplied, distinguished by title, and marks
   the second as "code to be confirmed".*
2. **`CAC. 708` repeats the title of the first `CAC. 701`** (*Fundamentals of Counseling Theories*).
   Either the two are the same course listed twice under different codes, or one title is a
   transcription error. The handbook treats them as distinct entries as supplied.
3. **Semester loading is asymmetric: 9 · 7 · 7.** If the intended pattern is 8 · 8 · 7, one 1st
   Semester entry belongs to the 2nd. This affects the part structure of the handbook.
4. **`CAC. 717 Research Methods in Counseling` and `CAC. 721 Research Methodology`** both fall in the
   3rd Semester and overlap substantially in title. Confirm whether both are required, whether one is
   an elective, or whether one title needs amendment.
5. **The unit value `1.6`** is uniform and unusual. It is reproduced exactly, as this repository has
   previously done for an irregular unit value, and no attempt has been made to round or restate it.

A sixth, purely typographic note: the supplied column heading reads "COURSE TITTLE". The handbook's
own tables are set as **Course Title**; the supplied spelling is preserved only in this source record.
