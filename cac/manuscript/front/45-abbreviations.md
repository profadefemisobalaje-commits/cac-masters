## Conventions used in this handbook

| Convention | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CAC. 7nn | a course code exactly as it appears in the University's curriculum record |
| Unit *n* | the *n*th lecture unit of a course chapter (six per chapter) |
| Lecture I, II, III | the three teaching sequences within a unit |
| *Hypothetical* / *fictitious composite* | case material invented for teaching; no real client is described |
| Illustrative image (AI-generated) | a photograph created for this edition; depicts no identifiable person |
| Jurisdiction-dependent | a legal or reporting duty that must be checked against local law |
