# Acknowledgements {.front}

A handbook of this kind is assembled rather than written, and the assembling depends on people whose names do not appear on it.

The University's curriculum record supplied the course codes, titles and unit values that govern the structure of this volume; they are reproduced without alteration. The academic registry answered questions about progression and assessment. The student support office specified the accessibility requirements the file had to meet, and was not satisfied by the first attempt.

Colleagues in the faculties of counselling, developmental psychology and education read individual chapters and objected to particular sentences, which is the most useful form of collegiality. Seminar leaders who have taught these courses at a distance described what students actually do when left alone with a text, and the units were revised accordingly.

The practitioners and supervisors who allowed their practice questions to be generalised into teaching cases gave more than they know; every case printed here is a composite and no one's client appears in it.

The typefaces used are EB Garamond and Source Sans 3, both released under the SIL Open Font License, and the diagrams were drawn for this edition.

Any error of fact, judgement or emphasis that survives all of this assistance is the editor's.
