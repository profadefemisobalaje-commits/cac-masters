# About This Book {.front}

**Triune Biblical University Global Extension USA** publishes this series as part of its doctoral curriculum in marriage and family therapy. The series translates each course of the doctoral programme into a full-length text: comprehensive in coverage, comparative in method, and written to the standard expected of doctoral work.

This volume corresponds to **MFT. 901 Advanced Theories of Family Systems**, the foundational theoretical course of the programme. It is designed to be used alongside the remaining volumes of the series, which address research methods, multicultural practice, clinical practice and assessment, psychopathology, ethics and law, human development, couple therapy, trauma, substance use, family life-cycle transitions, group work, neurobiology, supervision, mediation, parenting, integrative practice, technology, play therapy, spirituality in practice as an academic subject, advanced research methodology, and supervised practice.

**Scope and level.** The text assumes graduate-level preparation in the behavioural sciences or a related professional field. It does not assume prior training in family therapy, but it does not linger over elementary material. Terms of art are defined on first use and indexed thereafter. Statistical and methodological material is introduced where a theory's credibility depends on it.

**Structure.** The book opens with the historical and epistemological foundations of the systemic approach. It then works through the major theoretical traditions, devoting a section to each; compares them on common dimensions; examines the empirical evidence; and closes with the integrative frameworks that dominate contemporary practice and research. Appendices provide the standard notation for genograms, a comparative grid of theories, and a guide to using the volume in seminar and examination preparation.

**Learning features.** Each chapter contains:

- **Learning objectives**, stating what the reader will be able to do after study;
- **Numbered analytical sections**, each covering one tradition or theme;
- **Case illustrations**, hypothetical or documented, showing how theory appears in practice;
- **Key theoretical points**, summarising the central claim of each tradition;
- **Comparative perspectives**, placing theories side by side;
- **Applied exercises**, designed for seminars and supervision;
- **Assessment instruments**, describing measurement tools used in research on the theories discussed;
- **Doctoral research questions**, identifying live research problems; and
- **Further reading**, indicating where to begin with the primary literature.

**A note on academic honesty.** This book cites real works, summarises real research programmes, and identifies real controversies. It contains no invented quotations and no fictional attributions. Where a case is hypothetical, it is labelled as such. Where evidence is limited, the text says so. Where the field is divided, the text reports the division rather than resolving it silently.

**Professional notice.** This book is an academic text. It is not a clinical manual for unsupervised practice, it does not constitute clinical supervision or professional advice, and it does not by itself confer any academic qualification. Practice in marriage and family therapy is regulated differently across jurisdictions; readers must comply with the law and the professional requirements of the jurisdiction in which they practise, and must work within the limits of their competence.
