# Acknowledgements {.front}

A text of this kind is a synthesis of other people's work. The authors whose theories are examined here — Gregory Bateson, Don Jackson, Salvador Minuchin, Jay Haley, Mara Selvini Palazzoli, Murray Bowen, Ivan Boszormenyi-Nagy, Virginia Satir, Carl Whitaker, Paul Watzlawick, Sue Johnson, Leslie Greenberg, John Gottman, Norman Epstein, Donald Baucom, Andrew Christensen, Neil Jacobson, William Pinsof, Douglas Breunlin, Michael White, David Epston, Steve de Shazer, Insoo Kim Berg, Harlene Anderson, Harry Goolishian, Richard Schwartz, and many others — wrote primary texts that remain indispensable. Any summary is a simplification; the reader is urged throughout to consult the originals.

The editorial team thanks the faculty and examiners of Triune Biblical University Global Extension USA for their review of the curriculum design, and the doctoral candidates whose questions in seminars shaped the structure and emphasis of the chapters. Their persistent demand was for clarity: what does this theory actually claim, what evidence supports it, and what follows for practice? This volume attempts to answer those questions in that order.

The diagrams in this book were prepared for this edition. Photographs marked as illustrative images are AI-generated representations created for teaching purposes and do not depict identifiable persons, agencies, or events. Tables, exercises, and measurement instruments (where reproduced) are illustrative; readers requiring standardised measures must obtain them from the copyright holders or the public-domain sources indicated in the text.

Responsibility for the selection, interpretation, and any errors in what follows rests with the editorial team alone.
