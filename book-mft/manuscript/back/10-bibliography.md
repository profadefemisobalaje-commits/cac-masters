# Selected Bibliography {.back}

This bibliography lists the principal works on which this volume relies and the works recommended for advanced study. It is deliberately weighted toward primary sources: the founding texts of each tradition, the research programmes that tested them, and the critical literature that has disciplined them. Where a work exists in multiple editions, the edition most useful for doctoral study is given. Where an item is a journal article, the citation convention is author, title, year, and journal; readers should consult the source for pagination and for the full bibliographic details required by their institution's citation standard.

## 15. Professional and regulatory sources

Members of the profession must work within the law, ethics codes, and training standards of the jurisdiction in which they practise. The following are representative sources rather than an exhaustive list:

- American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy, *Code of Ethics* (current edition).
- Association for Family Therapy and Systemic Practice (United Kingdom), *Code of Ethics and Practice* (current edition).
- American Psychological Association, *Ethical Principles of Psychologists and Code of Conduct* (current edition).
- World Health Organization, *International Classification of Diseases* (current edition), chapter on mental and behavioural disorders.
- American Psychiatric Association, *Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders* (current edition).
- National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (United Kingdom), clinical guidelines relating to couple and family interventions.

Readers should verify the current edition and scope of every instrument, code, and classification cited in this volume before relying on it, and should note that some of the instruments discussed in the literature are proprietary and cannot be reproduced without permission from their publishers.
