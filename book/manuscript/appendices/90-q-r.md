# Selected International and Regional Judicial Decisions {.appendix letter="Q"}

This appendix summarises selected decisions of international and regional courts, tribunals, and quasi-judicial bodies, and leading national decisions with international significance, that are central to energy and natural resources law. Summaries are necessarily brief; readers should consult the full texts (Appendix P) and the chapters indicated.

# Professional Energy Law Drafting and Advisory Exercises {.appendix letter="R"}

**All exercises in this appendix are hypothetical. The states, companies, communities, and facts are invented for teaching purposes, and any resemblance to real persons, companies, or events is coincidental.** Where an exercise refers to real legal instruments, participants must rely on accurate, verified sources.

## Exercise R1 — Advice on Stabilisation (Advisory Memorandum)

The (fictitious) Republic of Tavora is negotiating a mining development agreement with Coreline Minerals plc for a lithium project. Coreline seeks a clause freezing the fiscal and regulatory regime for thirty years. The Ministry of Mines asks for advice. Prepare a memorandum (2,000 words) advising on: the types of stabilisation clauses; their enforceability and treatment in arbitral practice; their compatibility with Tavora's human rights and environmental obligations; and a recommended clause, with drafting.

## Exercise R2 — Drafting a Host Community Benefit Clause

Draft a clause (with explanatory notes) for inclusion in a petroleum licence in the fictitious Federal Republic of Ondara, providing for a community development trust funded by the licensee, with governance arrangements that ensure representation of women and youth, transparency, audit, and grievance procedures. Compare your clause with the host community provisions of Nigeria's Petroleum Industry Act 2021, identifying any improvements you propose.

## Exercise R3 — Force Majeure Notice and Response

The fictitious LNG buyer Eastgate Energy has received a force majeure notice from its seller, Coral Bay LNG, citing the imposition of sanctions affecting a shipping company it had chartered. Draft (a) Eastgate's response reserving its rights and requesting information, and (b) a memorandum analysing whether the notice is valid under a clause in the form set out in Appendix D, Part 10.

## Exercise R4 — Regulatory Submission on a Tariff Review

The (fictitious) Sunrise Distribution Company seeks a tariff increase from the state electricity regulator of the fictitious Kanem State, which has assumed regulatory responsibility under national legislation modelled on Nigeria's Electricity Act 2023. Prepare a submission on behalf of a consumer association, addressing cost reflectivity, affordability, service quality, lifeline tariffs, and the legal obligations of the regulator.

## Exercise R5 — Due Diligence Report

A (fictitious) private equity fund proposes to acquire a 40 per cent interest in Deltaflow Pipelines Ltd, which operates a crude oil pipeline in a fictitious West African state. Prepare a due diligence questions list and a report outline covering title and licences, regulatory compliance, environmental liabilities (including historical spills), host community relations, human rights, anti-corruption, contracts, disputes, and climate transition risk.

## Exercise R6 — Notice of Dispute under an Investment Treaty

The fictitious Solaris Renewables GmbH, a national of a fictitious state party to a bilateral investment treaty with the fictitious Republic of Mirandia, has seen its feed-in tariff retroactively reduced by 40 per cent. Draft the notice of dispute required by the treaty, and a memorandum assessing the strength of claims for breach of fair and equitable treatment and indirect expropriation in light of the jurisprudence on renewable energy support schemes.

## Exercise R7 — Legislative Drafting: Decommissioning

Draft the key provisions (no more than 1,500 words) of a decommissioning part of a petroleum bill for the fictitious Republic of Okanta, covering decommissioning plans, financial security, residual liability, the transfer of assets to the state, and re-use of infrastructure for carbon storage.

## Exercise R8 — Opinion on a Greenwashing Allegation

The fictitious Northwind Petroleum plc has advertised its LNG as "carbon neutral" on the basis of offset purchases. A consumer group has complained to the advertising regulator. Prepare an opinion on the legal risks and recommendations for Northwind's future communications.

## Assessment Criteria for Drafting and Advisory Exercises

| Criterion | Description |
|:---------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Legal accuracy | Correct identification and application of relevant law, verified from primary sources |
| Analysis | Identification of issues, risks, and options; balanced evaluation |
| Practicality | Advice and drafting that are workable, commercially realistic, and responsive to the client's objectives |
| Drafting quality | Clarity, precision, consistency, and absence of ambiguity |
| Ethics and integrity | Attention to professional duties, human rights, environmental and anti-corruption considerations |
| Presentation | Logical structure; concise and professional style |
