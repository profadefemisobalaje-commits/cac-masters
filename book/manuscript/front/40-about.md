# About the Author {.front}

**Triune Biblical University Global Extension USA** is the author and publisher of this work. The institution offers programmes of study and research through a global extension model designed to serve learners across multiple countries and regions, including professionals who combine advanced study with careers in government, industry, practice, and civil society.

This book was prepared as part of the institution's commitment to advanced scholarship in law and governance. It was developed as a comprehensive resource for doctoral and advanced postgraduate study in international energy and natural resources law, and for the professional development of practitioners, regulators, policymakers, and researchers working in the field.

The work reflects the institution's emphasis on:

- **Rigour** — grounding analysis in primary legal authorities, careful reasoning, and transparent research methods;
- **Global and comparative perspective** — examining the law of multiple jurisdictions and international regimes, with sustained attention to Africa and the developing world;
- **Practical relevance** — linking doctrine to the skills of drafting, negotiation, regulation, advocacy, and dispute resolution;
- **Integrity** — upholding the highest standards of academic honesty, including the accurate representation of sources and the clear labelling of hypothetical material; and
- **Public responsibility** — recognising that energy and resource law affects the welfare of societies and communities, and that its practitioners and scholars bear corresponding responsibilities.

As stated on the copyright page, this book is an academic resource. It does not by itself confer any academic qualification, and it does not constitute legal advice.
