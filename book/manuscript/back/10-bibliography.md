This bibliography lists principal scholarly and professional works consulted or recommended in this book. Primary authorities — treaties, legislation, cases, and awards — are listed in the tables in the front matter. Where a work appears in several editions, readers should consult the latest edition. Reports of international organisations that are published periodically are cited by series.
