**Coverage:** All 32 courses in the supplied curriculum, including electives; 16 weekly video topics per course (512 topics total).

These are draft topic titles and coverage anchors, not full lecture scripts. Course coordinators can adapt the sequence to the institution's semester calendar, local legislation, and approved readings. For OHSE.980, the weeks are dissertation milestones for supervision and research progress rather than a prescribed lecture series.
