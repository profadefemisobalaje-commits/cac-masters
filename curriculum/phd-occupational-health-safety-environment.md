# PhD in Occupational Health, Safety and Environment

## Full curriculum

Course codes have been normalized to remove spaces after the `OHSE.` prefix. The supplied heading typo “TITTLE” is corrected to “Title”; course titles and credit hours are otherwise transcribed from the curriculum provided. Every listed course carries 3 credit hours. Electives are kept separate from the year-by-year course plan because no elective year or selection rule was specified.
