The course

Energy and natural resources are the material basis of every economy, and the law governing them is the most consequential body of legal rules that most lawyers never study. This series takes the field in four movements: the foundations of sovereignty, trade and investment; the commodity regimes of petroleum, gas, electricity, the sea, mining and nuclear energy; the cross-cutting themes of the energy transition, climate change, human rights and the environment; and the settlement of disputes together with the distribution of the benefits and burdens of extraction. Every lecture is a complete thirty-minute class with slides, narration and subtitles, and each closes with a case, a set of readings, and differentiated objectives for undergraduate and postgraduate students.
